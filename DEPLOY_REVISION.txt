Companion emotional voice V17.20: public/companion-voice-suite-v1711.js + public/companion-voice-suite-v1711.css?v=20260823-v17.20-emotional-voice-system
Default voice: Grok TTS + Eve at 0.95x replaces the previous flatter Ara/1.00-style path. The same default is also enforced in src/tts.js through DEFAULT_GROK_VOICE="eve".
Per-character voice profile: each companion stores one shared voice profile used by auto-read, manual preview/replay and explicit call mode. Normal chat and calls no longer maintain independent voice identities.
Voice personas: Sweet Companion (Eve/.95), Gentle Healing (Ara/.92), Natural Daily (Sal/.97), Mature Gentle (Ara/.89), Lively (Eve/1.01), plus manual custom voice/rate/engine controls.
Speech modes: Natural Companion is default; Dialogue Only removes roleplay/actions; Full Read keeps all readable text. Natural Companion converts bracket/parenthetical/starred roleplay actions into pauses instead of literally reading stage directions.
Legacy migration: V17.16 profiles that do not contain voiceId/persona/engine are normalized to the new Eve/Grok/.95 defaults while preserving the user enabled state.
Emotional planning: responses are split into short expressive segments (maximum about 138 characters rather than the old ~520-character blocks). happy/shy/caring/sad/angry/thinking each adjust rate, inter-sentence pause and mouth intensity while preserving one consistent character voice.
TTS transport: active neural requests explicitly send both engine and voice_id. Grok remains preferred; backend can fall back to Melo and the browser system voice remains the final playback fallback.
Playback: gesture-unlocked WebAudio remains primary with a persistent <audio> fallback. Preview is available from the Emotional Voice panel.
Live2D synchronization: every speech segment applies the detected expression and drives ParamMouthOpenY/compatible mouth parameters for the duration of that segment.
